01 — The Gathering

Once a year,
the whole chain
in one room.

വർഷത്തിൽ ഒരിക്കൽ

Kerala Origins brings producers, harvest workers, processors, roasters, researchers, café teams and coffee people together once a year — in the same room, at the same tables, tasting the same coffees.

Most coffee grown in Kerala leaves the state as an anonymous lot. It is blended, re-bagged and sold under someone else's name. The grower becomes a line on a purchase order.

This gathering puts that back in order. Every lot on the table is traced to the farm, the elevation and the person who processed it — and that person is present, answering questions.

People. Place. Process.

People

behind the cup

മനുഷ്യർ

Place

Farms, districts,
elevations

ഇടം

The Western Ghats, shade systems, pepper vines, rainfall and soil — the conditions that make the cup what it is.

Process

Harvest to

രീതി

Picking, fermentation, drying beds and roast decisions — shown and argued over, not summarised on a label.
