Why this exists

Grown here.
Named elsewhere.

വളരുന്നത് ഇവിടെ

Most coffee grown in Kerala leaves the state as an anonymous lot. It gets blended, re-bagged, and sold under a name that belongs to somebody else. The grower is a line item on a purchase order.

Kerala Origins puts that back in order. Every lot on the table is traced to the estate, the elevation, and the person who processed it — and that person is standing next to it, answering questions.

No trade stand. No panel of buyers explaining the farmer's own coffee to them. Just the growers, the roasters who work with them, and enough cupping bowls to settle any argument.

The framework

People. Place. Process.

People

The hands
behind the cup

മനുഷ്യർ

Producers, harvest workers, sorters and processors — named, present, and speaking for their own coffee.

Place

Farms, districts,
elevations

ഇടം

The Western Ghats, shade systems, pepper vines, rainfall and soil — the conditions that make the cup what it is.

Process

Harvest to roast,
in the open

രീതി

Picking, fermentation, drying beds and roast decisions — shown and argued over, not summarised on a label.
