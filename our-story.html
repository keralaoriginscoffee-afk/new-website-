Our community · Kerala specialty coffee

Kerala coffee deserves to be known.

കേരളത്തിന്റെ കാപ്പിക്കഥകൾ

Kerala’s coffees deserve to be recognised for their quality, their origins and the people who make them possible. Kerala Origins brings people together to explore that potential and help it reach a wider world.

A shared purpose

Helping Kerala coffee find its place.

Kerala has long been a coffee-growing state. We want that contribution to translate into greater recognition for individual farms and the coffees they produce, with the people behind each harvest visible in the story.

Our work brings producer knowledge and roasting experience into the same conversation. Tasting carefully, sharing information about each lot and listening to one another help us understand what a coffee can offer.

Kerala Origins is a community initiative and a movement for Kerala specialty coffee. Producers, roasters, brewers and coffee lovers contribute different experiences, with a shared interest in helping these coffees be understood and valued.

From belief to practice

Taste together. Learn together. Build recognition.

Our work begins with producers and their coffees. Conversations about harvesting, processing and drying help us understand a lot before it reaches the cupping table. Roasting and tasting give us a way to discuss its character and consider practical next steps.

We welcome producers who want to explore their coffee’s potential, including those taking their first steps towards specialty. The support begins with understanding the farm, the coffee and the help needed.

By sharing that work with roasters, brewers and buyers, we aim to create more opportunities for producers and more reasons to seek out coffee from Kerala.

Shared knowledge

From the farm to the tasting table.

The work draws on roasting and tasting experience shared by Vajid KV of Bermito Coffee Roasters, alongside producer knowledge from Ephrem Joseph of TerraVagamon Conservancy, Moideen Kalladi of Silent Valley Estate and Nihal Parambath of On Ground Farm. These contributions sit within a wider community of growers, brewers, café teams and collaborators.

Sample roasting and cupping help us discuss what is in a coffee. Information from the farm gives that discussion context. Sharing both makes it easier to consider practical next steps and give producers clear credit when their coffees are presented.

Rooted here. Looking outward.

From Kerala’s farms to a wider coffee community.

Our annual programme centres on two harvest-season showcases: Kerala Origins Kochi and Kerala Origins Kozhikode. Each is a place to meet, taste and learn about the coffees and the people behind them.

Beyond Kerala, our ambition is to bring these coffees to festivals, roasters and buyers across India and internationally. The aim is lasting recognition for Kerala as an origin, with producers at the heart of that recognition.
