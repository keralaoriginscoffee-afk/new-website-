KERALA ORIGINS — COMMUNITY AND JOURNAL UPDATE
Prepared 13 November 2026

This ZIP contains 16 new or changed website files and this note. It is an update
to the existing keralaoriginscoffee.com website, not a complete replacement site.
The baseline was downloaded from the live site on 13 November 2026. The older
attached Index.txt was reviewed but was not used to overwrite the newer live site.

MANUAL UPLOAD
1. Extract the ZIP on your computer. Keep a backup of the existing website files.
2. Open the GitHub repository that serves keralaoriginscoffee.com. Use the same
   website directory that already contains index.html, styles.css and app.js.
3. Upload the 16 website files from this ZIP into that directory, replacing files
   with the same names. Add kozhikode-coffee-weekend-2026.html as a new file.
4. Leave existing images, assets, hosting settings and other files in place.
   UPLOAD-AND-AUDIT.txt is a handover note; it need not be uploaded to the site.
5. Commit the upload and let the existing hosting deployment finish.
6. Open the homepage, Journal, new article and Our Story. Refresh the page if an
   older cached copy appears. Updated CSS/JS references use v=20261113.

No live publication or GitHub changes were made during this task.

AUDIT FINDINGS AND CHANGES
- The live homepage, Our Story, FAQ, producer directory, older guide and event
  archives contained founder/ownership wording. Founder cards and titles were
  removed, including founder properties in structured data.
- Our Story now explains a shared community purpose. One paragraph acknowledges
  Wajid K.V., Ephrem Joseph, Moideen Kalladi and Nihal Parambath through their
  contributions. Producers retain their natural farm credits in the directory.
- Bermito is credited for roasting, sample evaluation, cupping and collaboration.
  The website does not present Kerala Origins as its subsidiary or commercial
  property, or claim a legal society registration that has not been supplied.
- Journal previously existed but was only linked from the footer. It now has a
  visible header shortcut and main-menu entry on every page, plus a homepage
  feature. Events stays out of the main menu and remains available in the footer.
- Added the full November coffee-weekend article with source links, reading
  contents, BlogPosting/Breadcrumb metadata and its sitemap entry. The existing
  Kerala regions/producers guide is retained and its community wording updated.
- The new article covers the wider community from Kerala and Bengaluru, festival
  programmes, cafe collaborations, NAS x Jaseem, Wayanad espresso and pumpkin
  coffee. Public programme details and team-supplied accounts are distinguished
  in the article's sourcing note. No results, recipes or attendance were invented.
- Automatic video, parallax, ticker animation, reveal effects and pause/resume
  controls were removed. Existing video posters provide static backgrounds.
- The contact bus illustration links to contact options without horn audio or
  motion. An obsolete hidden ticket link was removed from keyboard navigation.
- Menu focus handling, Escape, gallery controls and producer filters are retained.
- Existing green/paper palette, typography, branding and photo galleries retained.
