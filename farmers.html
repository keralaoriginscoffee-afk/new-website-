The wall chart · for anyone new to specialty coffee

Cherry to cup.

ചെറിയിൽ നിന്ന് കപ്പിലേക്ക്

Specialty coffee is ordinary coffee with nothing hidden: every step done carefully, measured, and traceable to the person who did it. Here is the whole journey, painted plainly.

Pick✹Ferment✹Dry✹Roast✹Cup✹കാപ്പിയുടെ യാത്ര✹
01Grow

It starts as a fruit on a tree.

വളർത്തൽ

Coffee is a cherry, grown under shade in the Western Ghats between roughly 700 and 1,600 metres. Elevation changes everything — higher and cooler means slower ripening and more flavour packed into the seed.

700–1,600 mKerala rangeShadePepper & jack
02Pick

Only the ripe ones.

വിളവെടുപ്പ്

Specialty picking means selecting cherry by cherry at full ripeness, by hand, over multiple passes through the season. It is slow, skilled work — and it is why the person picking matters as much as the plant.

03Ferment

Where the magic is decided.

പുളിപ്പിക്കൽ

The fruit is removed — or left on — and the seeds ferment for hours or days. Washed, natural, honey, anaerobic: these words on a bag describe exactly this step. Same cherry, different process, completely different cup.

96–120 hrLong ferments
04Dry

Weeks on raised beds.

ഉണക്കൽ

The seeds dry slowly on raised beds, turned by hand, protected from monsoon rain, until the moisture is exactly right. Rush this and everything before it is wasted.

05Roast

Minutes that reveal months.

വറുക്കൽ

A roaster takes the green seed and, in about ten minutes of heat, decides how much of the farm’s work you will taste. Specialty roasting aims to reveal the origin — not to bury it under darkness.

06Cup

Tasted, scored, argued over.

രുചിക്കൽ

Cupping is the standard tasting ritual: same recipe, bowls side by side, scored blind. It is how quality is agreed — and at Kerala Origins, the grower is at the table when their coffee is scored.
