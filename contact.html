Common questions

Before you write.

What is Kerala Origins?

A gathering held once a year for the people behind Kerala coffee. Growers, roasters and cuppers meet around traceable lots — every coffee on the table linked to its estate, its elevation and the person who processed it, with that person present to answer for it.

When and where is Edition 02?

Saturday, 11 October 2026, at The Cochin Club — 7, St Francis Church Road, Fort Kochi, Kochi 682001, opposite St Francis Church.

Who should come?

Coffee growers of any size, roasters, baristas, buyers, and anyone in or curious about specialty coffee. You do not need to work in the industry to attend.

How do I register?

I farm a small plot. Can you help me reach specialty grade?

Yes — that is a core part of what we do, and the size of your farm is not a barrier. We work alongside growers on guidance, consulting, processing, roasting and sampling, from harvest through to the cup. Write to us and tell us about your plot.

Where in Kerala does the coffee come from?

Chiefly Wayanad, Idukki and Palakkad, grown under shade in the Western Ghats between roughly 700 and 1,600 metres. You can follow the whole journey on the cherry to cup page.
