Why add your farm?

Named, not anonymous

Your coffee leaves Kerala under somebody else’s name. Here it carries yours — estate, elevation, and the person who processed it.

Seen by the trade

Roasters, buyers and cuppers come here looking for Kerala lots. However small your plot, you can be one of the farms they find.

Support, if you want it

Guidance, consulting, processing, roasting and sampling — we work alongside growers taking their coffee to specialty grade.
