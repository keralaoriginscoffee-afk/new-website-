Kochi.

A showcase & cupping table for Kerala’s quality coffees.

Date
Saturday, 10 October 2026
Time
2:00–6:00 PM IST
Venue
Cochin Club, Fort Kochi

Meet the growers behind Kerala coffee and taste their work at the same table. Our second edition brings producers, roasters and curious coffee drinkers to Cochin Club for an afternoon of cupping and conversations about how coffee is grown and processed.

Whether you work with coffee or are tasting it this way for the first time, come with questions. The focus is on the coffees, their origins and the people who know them best.

It started in
Kozhikode.

Date
Saturday, 11 July 2026
Time
2:00–5:00 PM IST
Venue
Sukoru Specialty Coffee, Calicut

The first Kerala Origins gathering brought Kerala-grown coffee into a shared conversation in Kozhikode. At Sukoru, coffee producers and drinkers explored Arabica, Robusta and Excelsa through guided cupping, producer talks and a brew bar.

Each coffee offered a way to discuss its farm, variety and processing choices. The table featured coffees from Terra Vagamon Conservancy, On Ground, Silent Valley Coffee, Puliyarmala Estate and Windermere Estate in Munnar.
