Rooted in the harvest

Two cities. A continuing conversation.

Our annual programme centres on two showcases across each Kerala coffee harvest season: Kerala Origins Kochi and Kerala Origins Kozhikode. Each city gives producers another place to share their coffees and the work behind them.

Looking beyond Kerala

Bring the origins with the coffee.

Our ambition is to take Kerala-grown coffees to coffee festivals and buyers elsewhere in India and around the world, with clear credit to the people, places and processes behind them.
