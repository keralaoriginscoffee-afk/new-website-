Kerala Origins
Kochi.

An afternoon with Kerala coffee at Cochin Club, Fort Kochi.

On 10 October 2026, the cupping table at Cochin Club brought Kerala-grown coffees into conversation. Sample bags, bowls and spoons made space for tasting, questions and a closer look at the work behind each coffee.

Kerala Origins exists to give that work a place: the farm, the people growing the coffee and the choices made through processing and roasting. Kochi brought producers, roasters, brewers and coffee drinkers around the same table.
